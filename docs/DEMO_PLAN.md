# Data collection plan

72 demos: 3 scripted games from the normal starting position, then three short drills that fill in castling, captures, and the left arm. The robot plays Brown. Every task text is generated by the app's own prompt writer, so the model trains on exactly the sentences it will get during a game.

**Mix:** 10 capture, 4 castle, 9 fix, 49 move. **Arms:** 36 left, 36 right.

## For every demo

1. Do what the **Before** column says. White's moves are made by hand and are not recorded.
2. Paste the **Task text** into the dashboard's task box and press **Set task**. Check it took.
3. Arms at their start pose, still. Press **A** to start recording.
4. Demonstrate it cleanly with the named arm (rules below), return to the start pose, then press **A** to save.
5. Anything went wrong? Press **B** to throw it away, put the board back, and redo it.

## What makes a clean demo

- **Only the named arm moves.** Keep the other one still at its start pose.
- **Approach from straight above** the piece, then descend.
- **Grasp only that piece**, by its body, never two pieces at once. Don't regrasp.
- **Lift high before moving across the board**, well above the tallest piece (the kings), so nothing gets knocked.
- **Descend straight down** onto the center of the target square. Put the piece down upright, then open.
- **Lift straight up after letting go**, then return to the start pose.
- **Calm and steady**, about 10 to 30 seconds. Never shorter than 2 seconds.
- **Knocked something over, or put it on the wrong square?** Press **B** and redo it. Never save a bad demo.

Captures are two demos: first the white piece to the tray on that arm's side, then the brown piece onto its square. Fix demos start with a piece nudged one square out of place, and say only "brown piece", as the app's fix prompts do.
